A camera, a viewer, and a server that never sees the video

Everything here runs on this machine and your local network. The phone becomes the camera, this laptop becomes the monitoring app, and the live feed travels straight between them — exactly the split described in section 02 of the proposal.

Running the demo
  1. Scan the code above on the phone and accept the certificate warning.
  2. On the phone, press Start and allow camera and microphone.
  3. On this laptop, open the monitoring app. The phone appears under UID registry — click it.
  4. Watch connection path read DIRECT · LAN while media through server stays at 0 B.
  5. Press Capture on camera to write a still on the phone from the laptop, and Talk to speak through it.
What this proves, and what it doesn't

Proven: live video and two-way audio over a direct encrypted peer connection, remote capture written to the device's own storage, a UID registry, and a control plane that relays a few kilobytes of signalling and no media at all.

Not proven: both ends are browsers, not camera firmware, and this connects over one Wi-Fi network. Real units need the media stack on the SoC plus the STUN and TURN tier for connections that cross the internet. That is the Phase 1 work.
